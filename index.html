My words

To keep your iPhone and iPad the same: tap ①, pick your file in Shared Files (its words are added here; nothing is removed), then tap ② and save it to Shared Files, choosing Replace. The very first time, just tap ②. Someone else's file only adds their words when you choose “Add words from someone else's file”.

Claude API key

Used only in Speak, when a word or phrase isn't in the word list: “Translate with Claude” (English → Croatian) and “Translate to English with Claude” (Croatian you heard or read). The key stays in this browser on this device; it is never put in an export file. Create or delete keys in the Claude Console (console.anthropic.com). Each translation is a small paid request on your API account.

Limitations

  
    
  
    Vocabulary
    
  
    The Croatian–English word list comes from Tako Lako, an open Croatian textbook by Dr. Frane Karabatić: utexas.pressbooks.pub/takolako
    
  
    Tako Lako is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license (CC BY-SA 4.0). The words and English meanings were collected from the book's lesson Vocabulary pages and its Vocabulary index on 3 October 2026 and reformatted for this app. The word list in this app is shared under the same license.
    
  
    
  
    Other sources
    
  
    Entries marked “study notes – not from the book” come from personal study notes (including café and household phrases written with Claude from general knowledge). Words you save yourself are marked “added by you”.
    
  
    Your data
    
  
    Everything stays on this device: the word list is built into the app, and your own words and settings are kept in this browser's storage for this site. Use ② Save my file (in More) now and then as a backup; the file goes only where you save it. Speech recognition is done by the device's speech service (on iPhone and iPad, Apple's), which may send your audio to its servers. When you use Translate with Claude or Translate to English with Claude, the text in that field is sent to Anthropic's Claude API with your own API key; nothing else is sent.
