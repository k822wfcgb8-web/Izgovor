About Izgovor

  

  
Limitations

  
    
  
    Vocabulary
    
  
    The Croatian–English word list comes from Tako Lako, an open Croatian textbook by Dr. Frane Karabatić: utexas.pressbooks.pub/takolako
    
  
    Tako Lako is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license (CC BY-SA 4.0). The words and English meanings were collected from the book's lesson Vocabulary pages and its Vocabulary index on 3 October 2026 and reformatted for this app. The word list in this app is shared under the same license.
    
  
    
  
    Other sources
    
  
    Entries marked “study notes – not from the book” come from personal study notes (including café and household phrases written with Claude from general knowledge). Words you save yourself are marked “added by you”.
    
  
    Your data
    
  
    Everything stays on this device: the word list is built into the app, and your own words and settings are kept in this browser's storage for this site. Use Export my words now and then as a backup. Speech recognition is done by the device's speech service (on iPhone and iPad, Apple's), which may send your audio to its servers.
